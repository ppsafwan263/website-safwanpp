What local SEO means for a Kannur business

The aim of local SEO is to help nearby customers find your business on Google. It covers the map results people see when they search for things like “bakery near me” or “dentist in Kannur”. Those map results appear separately from the normal web links.

Local SEO for small businesses in Kerala starts with one free tool, the Google Business Profile. Google used to call it Google My Business, and many owners still use that name. Both names mean the same profile, which shows your address, hours, reviews, and photos.

Here is a made-up example. A bakery near Kannur town has a complete, verified profile. When someone searches “cake shop near me”, Google has plenty of accurate detail to match against that search. A bakery with an empty profile gives Google very little to work with.

Your business qualifies if customers visit you or if you travel to them. Google’s guidelines set out the eligibility rules.

How Google decides who ranks on Google Maps in Kannur

Google ranks local results using three main factors. Its help page names them as relevance, distance, and prominence.

Relevance measures how well your profile matches what someone searched for. Distance looks at how far you are from the searcher or from the place named in the search. Prominence reflects how well known your business is, based on things like reviews and links from other websites.

Google keeps the details of its algorithm confidential, so nobody outside Google knows how much each factor counts.

You cannot move your shop closer to every customer. You can improve the other two factors, and that is where your effort should go. Paid ads are a separate route, and my Google Ads services in Kannur page explains how they differ.

How to set up and verify your Google Business Profile in Kannur

Start by checking whether your business already has a profile. Search for your business name and city while signed in to your Google account. Sometimes a listing already exists and only needs claiming. If it does not exist, add it.

Next comes verification, which you need before you can edit details or reply to customers. Google decides which methods you see, and you cannot change them. Options can include a phone call or text, an email, a live video call, or a postcard. If video verification is offered, Google recommends it.

  1. Postcards take patience. Codes usually arrive within 14 days and expire after 30 days.
  2. Do not edit your business name, address, or category while you wait, because that makes the code useless. Requesting a second code also cancels the first.
  3. Never share your code with anyone. Google says it will never ask for it, not even for the person who manages your profile.
  4. After you submit the code, Google reviews your details for up to five business days. Verifying your website through Google Search Console can sometimes verify the profile instantly.

Shopfront or service-area business

A shopfront is a place customers can visit. Google expects permanent signage with your business name at that address. A service-area business travels to customers, like a plumber or a home tutor. It gets one profile and should hide a home address. Virtual offices and mailbox addresses do not qualify.

How to fill in your profile so Google understands your business

Google says businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to show up in local results. Work through each field below.

Business name

Use your real business name, exactly as it appears on your signboard, website, and bills. Google does not allow taglines, hours, phone numbers, or service and location details in the name. Extra words can lead to suspension. For a made-up example, enter “Malabar Sweets”, not “Malabar Sweets Best Cake Shop Kannur.”

Category

Pick the fewest categories that describe your core business, and make them as specific as you can. Google suggests testing each one with “this business is a”, not “this business has a”. For a bakery, choose Bakery. Adding a category for every cake you sell misses the point.

Address, hours, phone, and website

Enter the real address customers can visit, your regular opening hours, and a phone number your business controls. Google prefers a local number over a central call-centre line when possible. Add your website if you have one.

Description

Describe your services, products, and history honestly. Google does not allow links or sale prices in this field, so leave out lines like “Everything on sale today.”

Photos

Google lists photos and videos among its tips for improving local ranking. Use real images of your shop, your team, and your work.

How to get and handle Google reviews the right way

Google ties prominence partly to reviews. Its ranking page says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It gives no minimum number, so ignore any claim about a magic review count.

Ask real customers for honest feedback. Send a Google review link or show a QR code at your counter. Asking soon after a sale is common advice, because the experience is still fresh.

Never offer discounts or gifts in return for reviews. Google treats incentivised reviews as fake content and strictly prohibits them.

Reply to reviews where you have something useful to say. Keep your replies free of offers. For a negative review, thank the customer, explain what you can do, and invite them to continue by phone or email. Google notes that a mix of positive and negative reviews can look more trustworthy.

How your website supports your local ranking

Prominence also draws on links from other websites, so your site plays a part. Keep its details matching your profile. If your profile shows a Kannur address and phone number, your website should show the same ones.

Write a clear page for each main service. Each page should describe that service in unique words, not the same text with a new town name swapped in. This is practitioner advice and not a Google rule. My SEO services in Kannur page follows this idea, with one service tied to one place.

Google lists a great page experience among its standard SEO advice, so test your site in PageSpeed Insights. Google’s current responsiveness measure is Interaction to Next Paint. A good score means at least 75 per cent of visits respond within 200 milliseconds, according to web.dev. Older guides still mention First Input Delay, which INP replaced in March 2024.

How to check whether it is working

Open the performance report in your business profile. It shows views, calls, direction requests, website clicks, and messages, though only the ones that apply to your business. The data is available only for verified profiles, and search terms update at the start of each month, says Google’s performance help page.

SEO usually takes around 3 to 6 months to show strong results. That is a typical estimate and not a Google figure. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking, so be wary of anyone who does.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay Google to rank higher on the map?

No. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Focus on an accurate, complete profile and genuine prominence instead.

How long does verification take?

It depends on the verification method Google offers. Postcard codes usually arrive within 14 days and expire after 30 days. After submitting a code, Google may review your details for up to five business days.

Can I use my home address?

If customers visit you, your business needs to meet Google's shopfront requirements, including permanent signage. If you travel to customers, use a service-area profile and hide your home address. Virtual offices and mailbox addresses do not qualify.

How many reviews do I need?

Google gives no minimum number. Ask real customers for honest reviews, and never offer gifts or discounts in exchange.

What is the difference between local SEO and a Google Business Profile?

Local SEO is the work that helps nearby customers find your business in search and map results. A Google Business Profile is one free tool within that broader work.

Conclusion

Local SEO in Kannur comes down to five jobs. Claim and verify your profile. Fill in every field honestly. Collect genuine reviews. Keep your website details matching. Check your performance report every month.
