Meta Ads FAQs: Facebook & Instagram Ads in Kannur

How much do Meta Ads cost in Kannur?

Most businesses spend ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 a month on ads, plus my management fee. I'll give you the full figure before we start.

Are Facebook ads worth it for a small business in Kerala?

Yes, if you sell something people like to see. Leads can cost just a few hundred rupees each. For emergency-only services, Google Ads is usually the better choice.

How quickly will Meta Ads bring me leads?

Often in the first week. Leads become steadier and cheaper after 2 to 4 weeks, so don't judge your ads on day three.

Is boosting a post the same as running Meta Ads?

No. Boosting aims for likes and reach, with weak targeting, no lead form and very little tracking. That's why boosted posts rarely bring customers. Ads Manager gives you better targeting, lead forms, WhatsApp buttons, proper tracking and testing.

Should I run Facebook ads or Instagram ads?

It depends on your buyers. Younger, visual audiences suit Instagram, while families and older buyers in Kerala are still very active on Facebook. I usually start with both and let the results decide.

Do you manage both Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes. Both run on the same Meta system, so I manage them from one account with one budget.

Can you run Meta Ads in Malayalam?

Yes. I often write ads in Malayalam or a Malayalam-English mix, which tends to bring more WhatsApp messages and cheaper leads.

Do I need a website to run Facebook ads?

No. Lead form ads and click-to-WhatsApp ads work without one. A website that converts does help with online sales, and I can build one for you (link: /website-design-services-kannur/).

Do you only work in Kannur?

No. I'm based in Kannur, but I offer Meta Ads services across Kerala, from Kasaragod to Kochi, including Thalassery, Taliparamba, Payyanur, Mattannur and Iritty. I also help Kerala businesses reach customers in the Gulf.

Why hire a freelancer instead of a Facebook and Instagram ads agency in Kannur?

With a freelancer, you get direct contact, faster replies and usually lower fees. An agency makes more sense for large, multi-brand businesses.
