Why Google Ads works so well for Kannur businesses

Think about the last time you needed a dentist in Thalassery, a driving school in Taliparamba or a contractor near Kannur town. You picked up your phone and searched. Your customers do the same. Pay-per-click advertising on Google puts your business in front of them at that moment, when they are already looking, and you only pay when someone clicks.

That's the big difference from social media ads. On Instagram you interrupt people. With Google search ads, people come to you with their need already typed out: "best orthodontist in Kannur", "plot for sale Payyanur", "AC service near me". Show up there with the right message and the enquiry is half done.

Kannur also has its own way of buying, and good ads respect that. Many decisions here are made together with family in the Gulf, so a real estate or jewellery campaign often has to reach NRIs in the UAE, Qatar and Saudi as well as people at home. Searches come in English, Malayalam and Manglish. Demand jumps around Onam, Vishu, wedding season and the weeks before schools reopen. I plan campaigns around these things instead of copying a template built for Bengaluru or Delhi.

How I run your campaign, step by step

  1. A real conversation first. We talk about your business, your margins, your best customers and what a good lead looks like for you. No forms full of jargon.
  2. Keyword research for your area. I check what people in Kannur and Kerala actually type, the search volume and cost per click (CPC) for each keyword, and which searches show buying intent. Then I pick the right keyword match types (broad, phrase or exact) so your ads show only for relevant searches. I also add negative keywords early, so you don't pay for "free", "jobs", "course" or towns you don't serve.
  3. Account and tracking setup. Tracking is tested before a single rupee is spent. If we can't measure leads, we can't improve them.
  4. Ad copy that sounds like you. Clear headlines, real offers, your location and your number, in Malayalam or English depending on your customers.
  5. Launch on a controlled budget. We start small, check the search terms report every day in the first weeks and cut what isn't working.
  6. Weekly optimisation. Bid strategy, keyword match types, ad scheduling, location targeting, Quality Score, ad relevance and A/B tests on ad copy. Most of the results come from this part, and it's exactly what set-and-forget setups skip.
  7. Simple monthly reports. What you spent, how many calls, WhatsApp chats and forms came in, your cost per lead (CPL), your return on ad spend (ROAS) if you sell online, and what I'll change next month. In plain language, not a 30-page PDF.
