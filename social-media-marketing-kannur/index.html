The simple explanation

What is social media marketing, in simple words?

Social media marketing (people also call it SMM) means using apps like Instagram and Facebook to tell people about your business so they come and buy from you.

Think of it like a shop window. A normal shop window is seen by the people who walk past on the road. A good Instagram page is a shop window that thousands of people in Kannur can see from their phone, any time of day.

It's one of the most affordable kinds of online marketing in Kannur and Kerala. But just posting photos is not enough. You need the right posts, at the right time, for the right people. That's the part I take care of. Done well, social media builds brand awareness (people start to know and remember your name). It gets engagement (likes, comments, shares and messages). And it puts your posts in front of your target audience, which simply means the people most likely to buy from you.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

What does SMM mean?

SMM stands for social media marketing. It means promoting your business on apps like Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp to get more customers.

How much does social media marketing cost in Kannur?

It depends on how many posts, reels and platforms you need, plus your ad budget. Message me and I'll give you an exact monthly price after a short chat.

How long does it take to see results?

Most businesses see more profile visits in the first month and steady enquiries in two to three months. Ads can bring faster enquiries if your budget allows.

Do I have to pay for ads?

No, it's not compulsory. Regular posting can grow your page on its own. But a small ad budget helps you reach more people near you much faster.

Which is better for my business, Instagram or Facebook?

For younger customers, food, fashion and travel, Instagram usually works better. For families, older customers and NRIs, Facebook is still strong. Most Kerala businesses do best with both.

Can you post in Malayalam?

Yes. I write captions in Malayalam, English or a mix, depending on who your customers are.

Do you work with businesses outside Kannur?

Yes. I work with businesses across all 14 districts of Kerala and in the UAE. Everything can be done online through WhatsApp and video calls.

Will you buy followers for my page?

Never. Fake followers don't buy anything, and they can hurt your page. I only grow real followers who might become customers.

Do I have to give you my password?

No. I get access through Meta Business Suite as a partner. You stay the owner of your page and can remove my access any time.

Are you a social media agency or a freelancer?

I'm a freelancer. You get the same work a social media agency in Kerala would give you, like content, reels, ads and reports. The difference is you deal directly with me, and the price is usually lower.

Is social media marketing worth it for a small business?

Yes, if it's done regularly. Small shops in Kannur often get more enquiries from Instagram and WhatsApp than from newspaper ads or flyers, and for a lot less money.
