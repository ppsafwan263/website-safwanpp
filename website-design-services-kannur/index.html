Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Send me a message and I'll be happy to help.

How much does website design cost in Kerala?

It depends on how many pages you need and what your website must do. A simple business website usually costs less than an online store. Message me for a free quote and I'll share the full price before work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple business website usually takes about 1 to 2 weeks. An online store can take longer. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Are you a web design company or a freelancer?

I'm a freelance web designer in Kerala. You work directly with the person building your website, often at a lower price than a large web design company.

Do you only work in Kannur?

No. I'm based in Kannur, but I make websites for businesses in every district of Kerala, from Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website I build is mobile friendly. Many of your customers will visit from their phone.

Will my website show up on Google?

I build every site with basic SEO so Google can read it. To reach the top of Google for competitive searches, you may also need an ongoing SEO service.

Can I change the website myself later?

Yes. If you choose WordPress, I'll show you how to update your own text and photos.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your website and domain name belong to you.
