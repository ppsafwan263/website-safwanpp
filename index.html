Which is better for Kerala businesses: Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads works best when people are already searching for what you sell, like "driving school near me". Meta Ads works best for showing your products to people who aren't searching yet, like new jewellery or clothing. Many Kerala businesses get the best results by using both.
