Introduction: running ads in Kerala without wasting money

Running a business in Kerala and confused about where to spend your ad money? You are not alone. Every day, people in Kannur, Kozhikode, Thrissur, and Kochi ask the same question: Should I run Google Ads or Meta Ads?

I am going to break this down in the simplest way possible — like I am explaining it to a 12-year-old — so you can make the right decision for your business. No confusing jargon. No theory copy-pasted from textbooks. Just real experience from running ads for businesses right here in Kerala.

What are Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Google Ads shows your business when someone types a search like “buy fresh mangoes near me” or “best digital marketing company in Kannur.”

You only pay when someone clicks your ad. That makes Google Ads highly valuable for businesses that want buyers who are already searching for their service or product.

Meta Ads includes Facebook and Instagram. These ads show up while people are scrolling reels, stories, and social feeds. They are designed to help you build awareness, generate interest, and attract attention with photos, videos, and creative content.

“Simple way to remember it: Google Ads catches people who are already hungry, and Meta Ads makes people hungry.”

How do they actually work?

How Google Ads works

Google Ads works like an auction. You tell Google, “Every time someone searches for 'digital marketing in Kannur', show my ad — and I will pay ₹30 each time someone clicks it.”

The person searching was already looking for what you offer. That makes them a hot lead — someone very likely to call you or buy from you. This is called intent-based advertising.

How Meta Ads works

Meta has data about billions of people. They know your age, what you like, what you watch, where you live, what you buy. You can tell Meta, “Show my ad to married women aged 25–40 in Thrissur who are interested in jewellery.”

Your ad appears while they are watching reels or scrolling through stories. They were not necessarily looking for you, but your ad was so good they clicked anyway. This is called interest-based advertising.

How do people in Kerala actually use the internet?

This is the most important part. Kerala is not like the rest of India. Kerala has some of the highest smartphone and internet usage in the country. Here is what I have seen in real campaigns.

  • People in Kerala search a lot on Google before buying anything, especially for services like doctors, AC repair, coaching classes, real estate, and events.
  • But they also spend hours on Instagram and Facebook watching local content, reels, and influencer videos.
  • Kerala has a very high literacy rate and people do their research before purchasing. This is great news for advertisers because a well-placed Google Ad in front of someone searching “best wedding photographer in Kozhikode” is gold.
  • At the same time, Kerala’s love for visual content and reels makes Meta Ads extremely powerful for fashion, food, beauty, education, and lifestyle brands.

“In Kerala, the customer researches on Google and gets inspired on Instagram. A smart business uses both.”

Which works better for your industry in Kerala?

  • Hospitals & Clinics: People search “dentist in Kozhikode” or “best skin doctor Kannur” with urgent intent. Use Google Ads.
  • Jewellery Shops: Visual product and emotional purchase. Instagram reels and Facebook shopping ads work brilliantly. Use Meta Ads.
  • Coaching Centres: Parents search Google and students browse Instagram. Use both.
  • Construction & Real Estate: High-intent searches like “3BHK flat in Thrissur” or “building contractor Palakkad” — people search before they call. Use Google Ads.
  • Fashion & Clothing: Kerala’s fashion scene runs on Instagram. Reels, influencers, and story ads drive massive sales here. Use Meta Ads.
  • Restaurants & Cafés: Google for “biryani near me” searches, Instagram for food photos. Use both.
  • Auto Services: Car service, AC repair, electricians — people search when they need it urgently. Use Google Ads.
  • Beauty & Salon: Before-after photos, bridal packages, and reel content work extremely well on Instagram in Kerala. Use Meta Ads.

Budget: how much do you actually need?

Let me be very honest here. Budget is always the first question Kerala business owners ask. Here is the real picture.

Google Ads budget

For most service businesses in Kerala, you need at least ₹10,000–₹20,000 per month to see meaningful results. Why? Because keyword competition is high, especially in cities like Kochi and Kozhikode.

Example: “AC repair Kannur” might cost ₹40–₹80 per click. If you get 200 clicks, that is ₹8,000–₹16,000 just for clicks.

Meta Ads budget

You can start seeing results with ₹5,000–₹10,000 per month on Meta. The cost per 1,000 people reached is much lower, and lead form ads can bring you contacts for as little as ₹50–₹150 each in Kerala.

This makes Meta better for businesses just starting out with paid advertising.

My honest recommendation: Start with Meta Ads at ₹5,000–₹7,000/month to build awareness and get your first leads. Once you understand your audience, add Google Ads at ₹10,000+/month to catch high-intent buyers. A combined strategy of ₹15,000–₹20,000/month often works best for a complete plan.

Mistakes Kerala businesses make with ads

After working with businesses across Kannur, Kozhikode, and beyond, I keep seeing the same mistakes over and over. Do not waste your money on these.

  • Running ads without a good landing page: You spend money on ads, then people click and get confused or do not trust what they see.
  • Using the wrong platform for the wrong goal: If you need urgent leads, Google usually wins. If you need awareness and visual storytelling, Meta is stronger.
  • Choosing aggressive targeting too early: Narrow targeting can reduce your audience size before you discover what actually works.
  • Not measuring which channel gives the best lead quality: Some cheap leads are not worth much if they never convert.
  • Copy-pasting ad creatives instead of localising them: Kerala audiences respond better when the message feels local, human, and relevant.

Final verdict — what should you choose?

If your business needs immediate, high-intent leads, Google Ads is usually the better choice.

If your business needs awareness, reach, and visual brand growth, Meta Ads often performs better.

Here is my honest answer for most Kerala businesses:

  • Service businesses with buying intent: choose Google Ads.
  • Retail, fashion, beauty, food, and lifestyle brands: choose Meta Ads.
  • Businesses with long-term growth plans: use both, but in a smart sequence.

My strongest advice is this: do not choose based on hype alone. Choose based on your business goal, your customer behaviour, your offer, and the kind of sales cycle you have.
