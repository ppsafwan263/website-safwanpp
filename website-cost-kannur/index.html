How Much Does a Website Cost in Kannur in 2026

A basic business website in Kannur typically costs ₹15,000 to ₹40,000. A custom-designed website costs around ₹40,000 to ₹1,00,000 or more. An ecommerce store usually starts from ₹25,000 to ₹40,000, plus platform fees. Your final price depends on the number of pages, the features and the design work involved.

If your developer is GST-registered, expect 18% GST on top of the price. Always ask whether a quote includes GST.

Neither quote is automatically wrong. A cheap template site can work for a freelancer testing an idea. Problems start when a business expects that same site to bring steady enquiries.

You will meet three kinds of website sellers in Kannur.

  • Template sellers are fast and cheap. Expect little customisation and limited support.
  • Freelancers usually sit in the middle on price, and you talk directly to the person building your site. Quality varies a lot, so always check their live work.
  • Agencies charge more and have a team of designers and developers. They suit bigger projects, though you may deal with an account manager instead of the builder.

Compare what each quote includes, not just the final number.

What Affects Website Development Cost in Kannur

Every website is a mix of time, skill and tools. These are the things that push the price up or down.

  • Number of pages. Each page needs design, content and testing. A 5-page site costs far less than a 25-page site with a separate page for every service.
  • Template or custom design. A template is a ready-made layout. Custom design takes longer because every section is planned for your brand.
  • Features. Booking forms, price calculators, customer logins and product filters all add development time.
  • Language. A Malayalam and English website needs every page twice, plus a language switcher.
  • Platform. WordPress, Shopify and custom code each have different setup and running costs. You will find a comparison below.
  • Content and photos. Someone has to write the text and take the photos. If the developer does it, the price goes up. If you do it, plan time for it, because missing content is one of the most common reasons website projects get delayed.
  • SEO setup. Basic SEO means proper page titles, headings and image descriptions. Keyword research, local SEO and content planning cost extra.
  • Integrations. Connecting WhatsApp, Google Maps, a payment gateway or a CRM each adds work.
  • Deadline. A rush job usually costs more or gets less testing.
  • Experience. An experienced developer charges more but tends to avoid the mistakes that cost money to fix later.

What Should Be Included in a Website Package

A fair website quote should clearly list what you get. Check that it covers these basics.

  • Mobile-friendly design that works on phones, tablets and laptops
  • An SSL certificate, which is the padlock in the browser bar that shows the site is secure
  • A contact form and a click-to-WhatsApp button
  • Google Maps showing your location
  • Basic on-page SEO, such as page titles, meta descriptions, headings and image alt text
  • Google Analytics and Google Search Console set up so you can see visitor and search data
  • Image compression and a speed check
  • The admin login handed over to you
  • A short training session on editing your pages
  • Some support after launch, often around one month

One rule matters more than any feature. Your domain and hosting should be registered in your name, using your email address. If the developer controls them, moving to someone else later can become slow and expensive.

Does hosting cost extra?

Usually, yes. Some developers include the first year of hosting in the package and charge for renewal later. Ask who pays from the second year and how much.

Does a domain name cost extra?

Yes, but it is a small cost. The catch is renewal. A domain sold for around ₹199 in its first year can renew at close to ₹1,000. Always check the renewal price before you buy.

How much does website maintenance cost?

Expect roughly ₹500 to ₹2,500 a month, or ₹6,000 to ₹30,000 a year. The price depends on how many updates, backups and edits are covered. Get the scope in writing, because "unlimited support" rarely means unlimited.

WordPress vs Shopify vs Custom Website Cost Compared

The platform you choose affects both the upfront price and what you pay every month.

  • A WordPress website is built on WordPress, free software that runs a large share of small business websites. You pay for the design, setup and hosting.
  • A Shopify website is an online store built on Shopify, a paid platform made for selling products. Hosting is included in the monthly plan.
  • A custom website is coded from scratch for features that ready-made tools cannot handle.
CompareWordPressShopifyCustom
Upfront costLow to mediumLow to mediumHigh
Monthly costLow, mostly hostingPlan fee plus any paid appsHosting plus developer support
Easy to edit yourselfYes, with a little trainingYes, very beginner-friendlyDepends on how it was built

Shopify has a few running costs that new store owners often miss.

  • The monthly plan. The Basic plan costs roughly ₹1,500 to ₹2,000 a month in India, depending on whether you pay yearly or monthly.
  • Payment fees. Shopify Payments is not available in India, so stores use a gateway like Razorpay. Razorpay's standard rate is 2% per payment, plus 18% GST on that fee.
  • Shopify's own fee. On the Basic plan, Shopify adds 2% when you use an outside gateway. On a ₹1,000 order, total payment costs come to about ₹43.60.
  • Apps. Paid apps for reviews, WhatsApp messages or email marketing add to the monthly bill.

So which costs less? For most service businesses, WordPress is the cheapest to run. Shopify is the easiest choice for selling products online, even with the monthly fees. Custom code only makes sense when you need features the other two cannot provide.

What Different Kannur Businesses Usually Need

Your type of business decides which features matter. Here is a simple guide.

01

Local shops and showrooms

Four to six pages, a photo gallery, Google Maps and a WhatsApp button. The basic business website range usually covers this.

02

Restaurants and cafes

A menu page that is easy to update, with food photos, opening hours, location and phone or WhatsApp ordering. This also fits the basic range.

03

Clinics, lawyers, accountants and consultants

An about page with qualifications, clear service pages, an appointment form and reviews. Expect the basic to custom range.

04

Real estate businesses

Property listings with photos, filters and an enquiry form on each listing. This usually lands in the custom range, because listings need a proper system behind them.

05

Ecommerce and jewellery brands

A product catalogue, size or weight options, a payment gateway and delivery settings. Look at the Shopify or WooCommerce range.

06

Freelancers and startups

A one-page or three-page site is enough to start. You can add pages as the business grows.

How Much Should a Small Business Spend on a Website

For most small businesses in Kannur, a sensible starting budget is ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 for the build. Add roughly ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 a year for the domain and hosting, and more if you want a maintenance plan.

Start with the pages you actually need. Spend first on clear content, good photos and fast loading, because animations can wait. Pick a platform that can grow with you, so you do not have to rebuild in two years.

A website on its own does not guarantee enquiries. It works best alongside SEO, ads and a well-kept Google Business Profile.

Mistakes to Avoid When Hiring a Website Developer in Kannur

Watch for these warning signs before you pay an advance.

  • The domain is registered in the developer's name instead of yours.
  • You never receive the admin login.
  • The offer includes "lifetime free hosting". Someone always pays for hosting, and these deals often end when you need help.
  • There is no written scope listing pages, features and support.
  • Someone offers a "complete website for ₹3,000." This is usually a template with no support.
  • The developer cannot show live websites they have built.
  • Anyone promises a number one Google ranking. Honest developers cannot guarantee rankings.
