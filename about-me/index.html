About me

Who Is Safwan?

I got into digital marketing because I wanted to understand how businesses actually grow online. That curiosity turned into my work. Today I work as a digital marketing consultant in Kannur, helping businesses get found on Google, generate quality leads and turn website visitors into paying customers.

My hands-on experience started with an SEO internship at HACA, where I did keyword research and built backlinks to help client websites rank. Since then I've run Meta Ads for a startup jewellery brand, built websites, and managed campaigns for local businesses.

I offer SEO and local SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media marketing, website design and Shopify store development for small businesses in Kannur, across Kerala and in the UAE. Everything I do is measured by one thing: real business growth, not likes and followers.
