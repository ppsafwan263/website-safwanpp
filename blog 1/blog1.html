How Much Does SEO Cost in Kerala? A Clear Price Guide for 2026

Ask three providers in Kerala for an SEO quote, and you could hear ₹5,000, ₹25,000 and ₹80,000 a month. All three may call it the same service. For most small local businesses, agency price lists sit between ₹15,000 and ₹35,000 a month. The real question is what that money buys.

How much does SEO cost per month in Kerala?

SEO in Kerala usually costs between ₹15,000 and ₹75,000 a month for small and mid-sized businesses. Large ecommerce or statewide projects can go above ₹1 lakh. These figures come from prices Kerala agencies publish on their own websites. They are examples, not fixed market rates.

Why two SEO quotes for the same business can be so different

SEO is not a fixed product like a phone. It is a set of tasks, and each provider includes a different amount of work. These are the factors that change the price most:

Competition

Ranking a clinic for “dentist in Thalassery” takes less work than ranking a builder for “flats in Kerala.” More rivals usually mean more content and links.

Your website's condition

A slow or broken site may need repairs before SEO can help. That initial work can add cost.

The area you target

Targeting one town costs less than all of Kerala. Each extra location needs its own pages and research.

How much content you need

Service pages, articles and product descriptions take time to research and write well.

Who does the work

A freelancer, a small team and a large agency all price their time differently.

Ask each provider: When two quotes differ, ask for the exact work included. The cheaper quote may simply include less.

What your monthly SEO budget actually buys

These are typical scopes based on public package listings, not strict rules. Your needs and local competition will affect the right plan.

Under ₹10,000 a month

Expect basic upkeep: Google Business Profile updates, titles on a few pages and a short report. It may help a small shop in a quiet market, but is rarely enough for regular articles or link building. Be careful if someone promises “complete SEO” at this price.

₹10,000 to ₹25,000 a month

This range can suit a single-location business in Kannur. Look for local SEO, improvements to key service pages, one or two new articles a month, and reporting on calls, enquiries and search visibility.

₹25,000 to ₹50,000 a month

Expect broader work: technical fixes such as page speed and broken links, a steady flow of content and some link building. This may suit businesses serving several towns or competitive fields such as real estate or education.

Above ₹50,000 a month

Larger budgets usually fund wider content plans, ecommerce category pages and SEO across many locations. This level mostly suits online stores and companies targeting the whole state or country.

The same budget can bring very different results in different industries. ₹20,000 a month might be plenty for a bakery and too little for a hospital chain.

Freelancer, agency or in-house SEO: which costs less?

There are three common ways to pay for SEO. Each suits a different size of business.

FreelancerUsually lower than agenciesOne person, sometimes with outside helpSmall local businesses that want direct contact
AgencyFrom about ₹15,000 a month, based on published listsA team with separate skillsBusinesses needing content, technical work and links together
In-house hireA 2025 Kannur job listing offered ₹22,000 to ₹45,000 a month in payYour own employeeLarger firms with steady, full-time SEO work

Freelancer

A freelancer is often cheaper, and you speak to the person doing the work. The limit is time: one person may struggle with a large site or many locations. One national agency estimates that freelancers in India charge ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 a month, though it gives no source.

Agency

An agency costs more but brings several skills under one roof. You may deal with an account manager rather than the specialist.

In-house hire

An in-house hire gives you full attention. Salary is only part of the cost. Paid SEO tools, training and your own management time add more.

No option is always best. Match the choice to your size, budget and how much work your site needs.

Costs that SEO quotes often leave out

A monthly SEO fee is not always the full cost. Check whether your quote covers these items.

Ask for one written quote that lists every cost. That makes a fair comparison much easier.

  • Website fixes. If your site is slow or hard to use on phones, it may need work first. Our guide to website costs in Kannur explains what those fixes usually cost.
  • Content writing. Some providers charge per article. Techpullers, for example, lists content writing at ₹5,000 per page.
  • GST. SEO services in India carry 18% GST when the provider is GST-registered. A ₹20,000 fee becomes ₹23,600 with GST. Freelancers below the ₹20 lakh turnover limit may not be registered.
  • Paid tools. Some providers pass on the cost of SEO software. Ask whether tools are included.
  • Ad spend. SEO fees do not cover Google Ads or Meta Ads budgets. Those are separate. See our comparison of Google Ads and Meta Ads for Kerala leads.

SEO steps you can do for free before paying anyone

Google's own hiring guide says small local business owners can probably do much of the work themselves. These free steps cover the basics:

  1. Set up your Google Business Profile. This free listing shows your business on Google Maps. Our guide to Google Business Profile setup in Kerala walks through each step.
  2. Add your website to Google Search Console. This free Google tool shows which searches bring people to your site.
  3. Ask happy customers for reviews. Honest reviews help new customers trust you. Never pay for reviews or offer gifts in return.
  4. Keep your details the same everywhere. Your business name, address and phone number should match on your website, Google and social media.
  5. Read Google's SEO Starter Guide. It is free and explains the basics step by step.

These steps will not replace ongoing work in a busy market. They do give you a strong base and help you judge whether a provider is doing real work. For the next level, read our guide to local SEO in Kannur.

How long it takes before SEO starts paying back

SEO is slower than paid ads. Ads can bring clicks the day you start. SEO builds results over months.

In a 2017 Google video on hiring an SEO, Google's Maile Ohye said SEOs usually need four months to a year. That time covers making improvements and then seeing the benefit. It is still a useful guide today.

Local searches in a smaller market like Kannur often face fewer competitors than statewide searches. That can mean earlier movement, but it is never guaranteed. A new website, a tough industry or a site with technical problems can all take longer.

Judge progress by steady signs, not sudden jumps. Look for more impressions, more calls from your Google Business Profile and more website enquiries over time.

What a good monthly SEO report should show

A report should show what work was done and what changed because of it. Check that yours includes these items.

Rankings alone do not pay the bills. A good report connects the work to enquiries. If impressions grow but clicks stay low, read our guide on fixing impressions with no clicks.

  • Clicks and impressions from Google Search Console
  • Ranking changes for your main target keywords
  • Calls, direction requests and website visits from your Google Business Profile
  • Enquiries from your website, such as form fills or WhatsApp messages
  • A clear list of tasks completed that month
  • The plan for next month

Warning signs of a bad SEO offer

Google's guide to hiring an SEO lists several warning signs. Be careful if a provider:

  • Promises a number one ranking. Google says no one can guarantee this.
  • Claims a “special relationship” with Google or offers “priority submit.”
  • Will not clearly explain what they plan to do on your site.
  • Contacts you out of the blue saying your site is missing from search engines.
  • Talks about link schemes or submitting your site to thousands of search engines.
  • Wants full control of your accounts for an audit. Google advises giving only read access to Search Console at that stage.

A low price is not always a scam. Still, if the price seems too low for the work promised, ask exactly what you will get each month.

Questions to ask before you sign an SEO contract

Use these questions to compare providers fairly. Several come from Google's own hiring guide. A good provider answers clearly and asks about your business, customers and competitors.

  1. Can you show examples of past work for businesses like mine?
  2. Which keywords will you target, and why?
  3. What exact work will you do each month?
  4. What results do you expect, and in what timeframe?
  5. Will I own my website, content and accounts?
  6. What happens if I want to stop?

Choosing the right SEO budget for your business

SEO cost in Kerala depends on your competition, your website and your goals. Most small local businesses will see agency prices between ₹15,000 and ₹35,000 a month. Start with the free basics. Then compare quotes by the work included, not just the final number.

Frequently asked questions

Is SEO worth it for a small business in Kannur?

It can be if your customers search for your service on Google. SEO can keep bringing visitors without paying for each click. Start with the free steps above, then invest if competitors still appear above you.

Is ₹5,000 a month for SEO a scam?

Not always. It may cover basic upkeep for a very small business in a quiet market. It is rarely enough for content or link building. Ask for a written list of the tasks included each month.

Is one-time SEO enough?

A one-time setup can fix problems and build a strong base. Competitors keep improving, though, so most businesses need some ongoing work. In a quiet local market, a small monthly plan may be enough.

Does SEO pricing include AI search like Google AI Overviews?

It depends on the provider, so ask. Google's hiring guide lists optimising for generative AI as a real service and advises checking that recommendations match Google's official guidance. Our article on how Google AI search affects SEO explains more.

Do I pay GST on SEO services?

Yes, if your provider is GST-registered. The rate on SEO services is 18%. A GST-registered business can usually claim this as input tax credit. Check the details with your accountant.
