The foundations

What is SEO? A simple explanation

SEO makes your website easier for search engines to understand and easier for people to use. It has three connected parts.

01

On-page SEO

What is on your website: relevant keywords, clear titles, helpful content and useful links between pages.

02

Technical SEO

How your website works: page speed, mobile usability, crawlability, indexing and fixing broken pages.

03

Off-page SEO

Signals from outside your website, including relevant links from trusted sites and genuine customer reviews.

i

Picture your website as a shop. On-page SEO keeps the shelves neat and labelled. Technical SEO keeps the doors open and the lights on. Off-page SEO is your neighbours recommending your shop. A good plan considers all three.

SEO is evolving

SEO for AI search: AEO and GEO made simple

People also ask questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and use Google's AI experiences. Clear, trustworthy information helps people and search systems understand your business.

NameWhat it meansWhere you may show up

These are related practices, not guaranteed placements. No one can promise that a search engine or AI tool will feature a particular page.

Hire with confidence

Looking for the “best SEO company in Kerala”?

“Best” is easy to write on a website. Before hiring any SEO expert in Kerala, ask clear questions and look for specific, honest answers.

  1. Can you show me real results from a past client?
  2. What exactly will you do in the first month?
  3. How will you report progress to me?
  4. Do you promise a #1 ranking? Be careful if the answer is yes.
  5. Will I own my website, Google Business Profile and data?

What does an SEO freelancer in Kerala do?

An SEO freelancer helps improve a website's visibility in search and bring in relevant organic traffic. Work can include keyword research, website improvements, Google Business Profile optimisation and progress tracking. You work directly with one person.

How much do SEO services in Kannur cost per month?

Cost depends on your website size, competition and the work needed. After an initial website check, I'll share a scope and a fixed price for the agreed work.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO usually takes months rather than days. Some websites see clear growth in about 3 to 6 months, but timing depends on the starting point, competition and work involved. Local profile changes may show effects sooner in some cases.

Can you guarantee the first page on Google?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a specific ranking because Google controls its results. I can offer honest work, a clear plan and regular reporting.

What is the difference between local SEO and normal SEO?

SEO can help your pages rank for topics broadly. Local SEO focuses on helping nearby people find your business, particularly in Google Maps and “near me” searches.

Do I need Google Business Profile optimisation?

If customers visit your shop or office, or you serve a particular area, a complete and accurate Business Profile can help them find your details and contact you.

How do I rank my business on Google Maps in Kannur?

Start with a complete Business Profile, accurate categories, address and hours, and real photos. Ask customers for honest reviews, reply to them and keep your website's business details consistent. These are key local SEO fundamentals.

Do you offer affordable SEO services for small businesses?

Yes. Most of my clients are small and medium businesses in Kerala. I keep plans focused so you can prioritise the work that fits your business and budget.

Is SEO better than Google Ads for a small business?

They do different jobs. Google Ads can bring visitors while you pay for campaigns; SEO takes longer but can build organic visibility over time. Some businesses use both. I'll help you decide based on your goals and budget.

Can you get my business into ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?

No one can promise inclusion. I can make your website clearer, faster and more useful, with accurate business information and a strong local presence. These are sound foundations for search and AI discovery.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO improves visibility in search results. AEO focuses on clear answers that can be used in answer experiences. GEO focuses on making information understandable and useful to generative AI tools. A good page can support all three.

Do I need a new website for SEO?
